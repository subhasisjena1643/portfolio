Personal portfolio direction · Revision 2

## The opening

Set **Subhasis Jena** as the largest type on the page. Pair the name with the upper symbol Subhasis created and has chosen as his personal signature. Underneath, set “Designer & creative technologist · Bengaluru” in Manrope. Use Work, About and Say hello as ordinary navigation.

Suggested first-person copy, ready for the owner to tune:

> I design interfaces for things that are hard to see. A classroom session becomes a set of observations. PyTorch code becomes a graph you can inspect.

Beside the introduction, compose three fragments in one viewfinder. In order, show a ClassAnalyzer session overlay, an edge from the MuLM Studio demo graph, and a planar assembly diagram for Programmable Magnetic Assembly. Shared edges and a small registration notch make the three figures read as one print. Label the third fragment as research. “Selected work” leads directly to the featured projects.

## What a visitor can do

Each fragment has a visible title and a link. On a wide screen, an optional focus button can expand its crop. Preserve all three links and give the fragments equal width when the page opens. At phone widths, place three shallow figures in reading order with captions underneath. A keyboard or touch visitor can reach every destination directly.

## Why this direction fits

The portfolio connects interface engineering, model inspection and research. The public repositories establish the implemented behaviors shown in the two featured studies. The research source documents support questions and proposed tests. [ClassAnalyzer](https://github.com/subhasisjena1643/classanalyzer) · [MuLM Studio demo](https://github.com/MuLMStudio/demo)

The earlier portfolio research informed three choices: use a consistent typographic identity across routes, give interaction a subject people can understand, and pair each close-up with a caption explaining the decision. The implementation plan retains the reference audit. The specific crops, captions, illustration and arrangement come from this portfolio’s own material.

**Review question.** After seeing the homepage, can someone recall Subhasis’s name, describe two different kinds of work and choose a chapter without instructions? After reading one chapter, can they point to one choice he made?
